## 7. Positioning recommendations

1. **Own the niche verticals.** Build out `/industries/` — roofing, landscaping, restaurants are started; add HVAC, plumbing, electrical, concrete, etc. Each becomes a page targeting `[trade] web design` + `[trade] SEO`.
2. **Go local + service, not generic.** Layer city/region (Ohio, Cleveland, Columbus) onto the trade pages — `roofing web design ohio`, `landscaping website cleveland`. Low competition, high intent.
3. **Fix the one technical flag:** homepage (and any page) missing image alt text — quick win, helps image search + accessibility.
4. **Lean on the /insights/ blog** for the informational-intent gap keywords — checklists, guides, cost pages that earn links.
5. **Convert the 36 backlinks into 60+** via the case studies (/work/) — pitch to the featured clients and trade associations.
6. **Internal-link the money pages** from the blog and case studies to pass authority to /services/ and /industries/.
